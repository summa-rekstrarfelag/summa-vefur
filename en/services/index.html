Advisory

Summa’s Distinct Approach

Summa’s strength lies in the experience and expertise of its team, who focus on delivering value-adding, innovative, and reliable advisory services through careful analysis and disciplined execution.

Summa specializes in projects within the fields of economic and social infrastructure, where the team’s deep knowledge of project and structured finance and risk analysis provides a unique advantage. The firm has placed particular emphasis on renewable energy projects, efficient resource utilization, and solutions to pressing environmental and social challenges, such as those in the transportation sector.

Key areas of focus include:

  • Assessment of specialized investments and projects
  • Structuring of technically complex financial solutions
  • Comprehensive corporate financing strategies
  • Risk analysis and advisory on risk management practices

Financing

Summa provides comprehensive advisory services related to the financing of projects, developments, and companies.

The emphasis is on building financially sound structures that align with operational needs and stakeholder interests while maintaining resilience to risk.

The firm has participated in numerous projects across transportation, renewable energy, energy infrastructure, and social infrastructure—often involving complex risk analysis and tailored financing structures.

Summa’s advisory work spans:

  • Public-private partnerships (PPP) and private project finance
  • Private equity and co-investment structures
  • Corporate debt financing
  • Integrated capital solutions combining equity and debt financing

Summa’s team combines broad expertise in financing and risk analysis, ensuring that complex projects are executed successfully from concept to completion.

Investment Advisory License

Summa is authorized by the Central Bank of Iceland to provide investment advice under its operating license. According to Icelandic law no. 115/2021 on Markets in Financial Instruments, investment advice refers to “personalized recommendations to a client regarding transactions in financial instruments, whether at the client’s request or on the initiative of the adviser”.

Summa’s investment advisory services may cover both listed and unlisted securities, including equities, bonds, and more complex structured products.
